### 2.1 Parametric Face Models.
Linear morphable face models [ 7 , 19 ] and the encoder-based identity predictors built on them [ 47 ] derive their shape spaces via global dimensionality reduction, so their parameters are highly entangled and lack explicit correspondence with localized semantic attributes. While mapping linguistic descriptions to global shape spaces has been explored for parametric body models [ 35 ] , achieving localized, disentangled control over specific facial attributes on the FLAME manifold remains an open challenge. DeltaRegion addresses this gap by operating entirely inside the FLAME identity shape space, giving us a strong inductive bias for plausibility while enforcing localized, semantic control, and keeping every edit trivially compatible with downstream FLAME -rigged assets.
