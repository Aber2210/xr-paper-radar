Two agents interact in a shared Minecraft environment (Left).
In addition to generating each agent’s egocentric view (Bottom), OverSeer  models six fixed-pose auxiliary cameras (Right).
These auxiliary streams establish a persistent shared reference, enforcing world consistency over time and agent consistency across views. We structure our experimental evaluation to test the central hypothesis of this work: extending a multi-agent video world model with pose-fixed, efficiently represented auxiliary views significantly improves both temporal and agent consistency compared to baselines ( section   4.1 ).
We then conduct ablation studies investigating how performance scales with the number of auxiliary views and how the δ \delta -VAE compression ratio influences synthesis quality and computational overhead ( section   4.2 ).
For qualitative results, please refer to our website and appendix.
Environments and Benchmarks.
We evaluate world models by conditioning future frame rollouts on initial visual observations and multi-agent action trajectories. Primary quantitative evaluations are conducted in Minecraft , whose persistent and editable 3D world naturally tests spatial memory, multi-agent interaction, and cross-view consistency ( tables   1 and  2 ). To evaluate long-horizon spatial reasoning, we additionally run revisit tasks in Doom ( table   2 ) and showcase qualitative generalization on real-world video captures from Replay   ( Shapovalov et al., 2023 ) . In Minecraft, evaluation spans two benchmark suites:
1.
Solaris Benchmark   ( Savva et al., 2026 ) : Evaluates five multi-agent capabilities using VLM-judged scripted questions, requiring all questions within an episode to be answered correctly.
2.
MAC-Bench (Multi-Agent Consistency Benchmark) : Designed specifically to evaluate world and agent consistency in a harder setting. Specifically. we design the following three tasks:
•
Reunite at Start : Both agents return to their exact initial poses. Consequently, an agent’s partner must reappear at a previously observed spatial location.
•
Reunite Far : Agents navigate long, converging routes without visual contact in transit, turning to face each other upon arrival. A partner must appear at close range within previously unobserved space.
