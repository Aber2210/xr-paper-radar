The platform also integrates a real-time data collection pipeline that records both system-level and user-level metrics.
Captured data includes cognitive attack events, behavioral signals (e.g., head and eye tracking), user activity logs, and subjective measures such as engagement and trust.

4 Search-and-Rescue Features
This section describes the core search-and-rescue features supported by the CAESAR testbed and explains how they shape mission dynamics, user behavior, and experimental control. These components capture key elements of real-world search-and-rescue operations, including time pressure, environmental uncertainty, adversarial interaction, and team coordination, enabling systematic study of collaborative performance under stress.

( Left ): Unified health system linking hazards, combat, rescue, and recovery.

4.1 Health System
We introduce a unified health system to model survival constraints, time pressure, and resource dependency within the search-and-rescue scenario.
Health serves as a shared quantitative state for players, hostages, and adversaries, linking environmental hazards, combat interactions, and rescue urgency within a single framework (see Figure   3 (Left)).
Players experience gradual health decay over time and additional damage from environmental hazards and adversarial encounters.
Health can only be restored through limited recovery resources, creating strategic trade-offs between exploration, combat, and self-preservation.
Hostages also experience health degradation, with varying survival durations that require players to prioritize rescue decisions under uncertainty.
Adversaries possess health states that decrease through combat, enabling bidirectional risk during engagement.
Importantly, health directly constrains interaction capabilities.
When a player’s health is depleted, their ability to perform mission actions is restricted until recovery is achieved.
This coupling between physiological state and action capability introduces sustained cognitive and behavioral pressure, encouraging risk assessment, coordination, and adaptive decision-making throughout the mission.
