Table  I reports BD-rate results over the full Forward-facing class using GSCodec Studio as the anchor. CCT-GS achieves consistent bitrate savings on all tested sequences and obtains the best average BD-rate reduction of 51.30% relative to the anchor. The gains are observed across tracked, semi-tracked, and untracked sequences, demonstrating robust R-D improvements across different forward-facing 3DGS contents.
III-C Ablation Study
We conduct an ablation study on the bartender_tracked sequence to validate the contribution of each component. Direct HEVC denotes directly coding the SH-AC maps with HEVC. As shown in Fig.  3 , CCT-GS with MSE-only training (i.e., only generalized transform without LoRA-based adaptation) achieves an 11.40% BD-rate reduction over Direct HEVC, confirming the effectiveness of the learned SH-AC bottleneck transform. Introducing the rendering loss further brings a 3.13% BD-rate reduction over the MSE-only model, indicating that render-aware optimization better aligns the reconstructed SH-AC attributes with the final view quality. With LoRA-based scene adaptation, the full CCT-GS obtains an additional 22.72% BD-rate reduction over the generalized SH-AC transform without LoRA, and achieves a total BD-rate reduction of 37.75% over Direct HEVC. These results show that the generalized SH-AC transform captures cross-scene regularities of SH-AC attributes, while LoRA-based scene adaptation further learns scene-specific appearance details to improve R-D efficiency.
