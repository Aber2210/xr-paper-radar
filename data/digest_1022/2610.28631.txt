We present AEON, a generative world model that operates within the latent space of a pretrained video diffusion model while producing explicit 4D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS) as the world representation. Given a text prompt or conditioning image, our goal is to generate a set of 4D Gaussians encoding both appearance and dynamics, enabling photorealistic rendering and persistent 3D simulation. The overview of AEON is shown in Fig.  2 . We begin by introducing the 4DGS representation in Sec.  3.1 , which models dynamic scenes through velocity-driven motion and time-varying visibility. In Sec.  3.2 , we present the 4D Gaussian Reconstruction Model, whose four task-specific heads jointly predict depth, Gaussian appearance, dynamic evolution, and camera poses from monocular videos. We then describe our domain adapter in Sec.  3.3 , which bridges the generative video latent space and the reconstructive 4D latent space through distillation. Finally, Sec.  3.4 details our multi-stage training strategy that optimizes the full pipeline end-to-end.
3.1 4D Gaussian Splatting as the World Representation
An ideal world model should jointly capture realistic appearances, scene dynamics, and 3D-consistent geometry within a persistent and efficiently renderable representation. To this end, AEON adopts 4D Gaussian primitives  [ 41 , 53 , 60 ] as its foundational scene representation, where each primitive simultaneously encodes geometry, appearance, and temporal evolution. 4.1 4D Generation
We evaluate AEON against 4DNex  [ 7 ] and Lyra  [ 3 ] for 4D generation. Unlike 4DNex (per-frame point clouds) and Lyra (per-frame 3DGS), AEON predicts a unified 4DGS representation for real-time exploration. We evaluate on 30 scenes, each specified by an image-text prompt. Evaluation videos, rendered along shared camera trajectories from identical image-text prompts, are assessed via VBench  [ 13 ] metrics: I2V Subject, Subject Consistency, Motion Smoothness, and Aesthetic Quality. As shown in Table  2 , AEON outperforms all baselines. While per-frame reconstruction lacks cross-frame coherence, AEON models scenes via persistent 4D Gaussians with explicit velocity and duration, naturally ensuring temporal consistency. Additionally, AEON generalizes across backbones (Wan 2.1 and Wan 2.2) without architectural modifications, yielding consistently strong results.

【结论/局限】
 We present AEON, a unified world modeling framework that bridges video-based and 3D-based paradigms and combines the benefits of both. The core of AEON is a generative model that operates within the latent space of a pretrained video diffusion model while producing explicit 4D Gaussian Splatting as the world representation. We train a 4DRM for robust geometric guidance and design a domain adapter that translates generative video latents into the reconstructive 4D latents through end-to-end distillation. Experiments across 4D generation, dynamic-scene reconstruction, and novel trajectory rendering demonstrate that AEON achieves state-of-the-art performance while supporting a broad spectrum of world modeling applications within a single unified framework. and future work.
