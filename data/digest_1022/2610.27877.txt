During motion training, annotated intentions and fields predicted by the frozen ACG model provide semantic and geometric conditions.
The denoising objective penalizes discrepancies between predicted and target v v values, with supervision restricted to valid future state components:
ℒ v = ‖ 𝐖 fut ⊙ ( 𝐯 ^ fut ( τ ) − 𝐯 fut ( τ ) ) ‖ F 2 max ⁡ ( 1 , ‖ 𝐖 fut ‖ 1 ) , \mathcal{L}_{v}=\frac{\left\|\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{fut}}\odot\left(\hat{\mathbf{v}}_{\mathrm{fut}}^{(\tau)}-\mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{fut}}^{(\tau)}\right)\right\|_{F}^{2}}{\max\!\left(1,\|\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{fut}}\|_{1}\right)},
(11)
where 𝐖 fut \mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{fut}} is a binary mask identifying valid future state components across the minibatch, and ∥ ⋅ ∥ F \|\cdot\|_{F} and ∥ ⋅ ∥ 1 \|\cdot\|_{1} denote the Frobenius and entrywise L 1 L_{1} norms, respectively.
To encourage coordinated and physically plausible interactions, we apply auxiliary losses to denormalized clean-state estimates recovered from the predicted v v .
The overall objective is
ℒ motion = \displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{motion}}={}
ℒ v + ℒ geo + ℒ rel + ℒ contact \displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{v}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{geo}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rel}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{contact}}
(12)
+ ℒ obj + ℒ sup + ℒ move , \displaystyle+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{obj}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sup}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{move}},
where ℒ geo \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{geo}} supervises translations, velocities, and rotations, while ℒ rel \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rel}} constrains relative hand-object motion by matching wrist-relative poses and their temporal changes.
Contact consistency and collision avoidance are encouraged through ℒ contact \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{contact}} , which combines regional contact-distance matching with hand-object penetration penalties, and ℒ obj \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{obj}} , which penalizes object-object penetration.
The support term ℒ sup \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sup}} addresses furniture penetration, terminal placement, and placement-goal errors, while ℒ move \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{move}} supervises object transport relative to the intended target.
Each auxiliary term uses its corresponding validity mask.

【实验】
on EgoAIM demonstrate improvements over the evaluated baselines in both intention inference and interaction forecasting, supporting the complementary contributions of gaze information and geometric grounding.
End-to-end performance approaches the levels achieved under oracle conditioning in specific phases, while precise placement remains an important direction for future improvement.
By connecting inferred interaction goals with their anticipated spatial realization, this work provides a foundation for advancing proactive human-centric assistance in AR, VR, and embodied systems.
