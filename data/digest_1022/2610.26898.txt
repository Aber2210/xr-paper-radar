Given a single source image 𝐈 s \mathbf{I}_{s} , our goal is to reconstruct a photorealistic head of an unseen subject. To tackle this non-trivial task, we employ the 3 3 D Gaussian Splatting ( 3 3 D-GS) pipeline  [ 45 ] . 3 3 D-GS models a scene with a set of 3 3 D Gaussians, each defined by a 3 3 D covariance matrix Σ \Sigma and a center (mean) position p p :

Each Gaussian 𝐆 \mathbf{G} is parameterized by 𝒢 = { 𝐩 , 𝐪 , 𝐬 , α , 𝐜 } \mathcal{G}=\{\mathbf{p},\mathbf{q},\mathbf{s},\alpha,\mathbf{c}\} where 𝐩 ∈ ℝ 3 \mathbf{p}\in\mathbb{R}^{3} is the center position, 𝐪 ∈ ℝ 4 \mathbf{q}\in\mathbb{R}^{4} is the rotation quaternion, 𝐬 ∈ ℝ 3 \mathbf{s}\in\mathbb{R}^{3} is the scaling factor, α ∈ ℝ 1 \alpha\in\mathbb{R}^{1} is the opacity, and 𝐜 ∈ ℝ 3 \mathbf{c}\in\mathbb{R}^{3} is the color.

4.2 Reconstruction Quality

【实验】
4.1 Experimental Setup
Datasets and Baselines. 4.1 Experimental Setup
Datasets and Baselines.
We train on 30,000 30,000 FFHQ  [ 44 ] images. For evaluation, we use a disjoint set of 1,000 1,000 FFHQ images and 1,000 1,000 CelebA-HQ  [ 43 ] images. To train our density prediction network, we generate pseudo ground-truth Gaussian center maps for all training images using our proposed GAM (Sec.  3.1 ).
For the baselines, we compare EGGHead’s Gaussian representation quality against single-image head reconstruction methods, including the 3D-aware GANs E3DGE  [ 52 ] and G-NeRF  [ 98 ] , as well as the 3D-GS based method GAGAvatar  [ 17 ] , using the same M M = 12 12 k Gaussians setting where applicable.

【结论/局限】
 We’ve proposed EGGHead, a single-image generalizable head modeling method that offers fast enrollment and rendering while maintaining high reconstruction quality. Our Gaussian allocation algorithm assigns appropriate numbers of Gaussians to different semantic head regions, preserving visual fidelity even with lower Gaussian budgets. Our feed-forward design combines a Gaussian allocation and density prediction network with a Gaussian attribute regressor, eliminating expensive per-subject optimization typically required in 3D-GS. In addition, Voronoi tessellation-guided attribute prediction improves structural accuracy under sparse single-view settings, while the Face Symmetry Module enhances completeness without additional heavy networks. We hope our method encourages future work on efficient, high-quality, and generalizable Gaussian heads from single images. .
