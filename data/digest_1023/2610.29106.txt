3.1. Preliminaries: Neural Radiance Fields
Given a sparse set of posed images { I 1 , … , I N } \{I_{1},\ldots,I_{N}\} of a single scene, our goal is to synthesize novel views from unseen camera poses.
Following standard practice  ( Mildenhall et al., 2020 ) , we optimize a neural radiance field per scene and query it at test time.

4.5 Dense-View Behavior 4.1. Setup
We evaluate on NeRF-Synthetic  ( Mildenhall et al., 2020 ) , 15 DTU scans  ( Jensen et al., 2014 ) , and eight LLFF scenes  ( Mildenhall et al., 2019 ) under 3-, 6-, and 9-view protocols.
The bundled loaders reserve every eighth camera for testing on DTU and LLFF and select sparse training cameras evenly from the remainder; NeRF-Synthetic uses evenly spaced training frames and all test frames.
Table   1 gives the rest of the protocol, including coordinate conventions and near/far bounds; no NDC transform is applied on any dataset, and the drifting distance is evaluated in these loader coordinates with no additional scene-scale normalization.
Our MLP has eight layers of width 256, a skip after layer 4, 10/4 positional-encoding octaves for position/direction, softplus density, 1024 rays per minibatch, and 200k Adam steps with learning rate 5 × 10 − 4 5\times 10^{-4} decayed exponentially to 5 × 10 − 5 5\times 10^{-5} .
Our rows average seeds 0, 1, and 2. PSNR, TorchMetrics SSIM  ( Wang et al., 2004 ) , and LPIPS-Alex  ( Zhang et al., 2018 ) are computed for every held-out view and then averaged; the ten-minibatch Lightning validation pass is used only for progress monitoring.
Every number we report was recomputed under Table   1 rather than copied from a published table.
This matters for absolute comparability: the sparse-view literature scores DTU with object masks and LLFF in normalized device coordinates, whereas our loaders do neither.
Masked DTU evaluation discards exactly the background region that sparse-view methods reconstruct worst, so published DTU numbers sit well above ours at the same view count; FreeNeRF, for instance, reports 19.92 19.92  dB on masked DTU 3-view against the 17.89 17.89  dB it reaches here.
Values in this paper are therefore internally comparable across rows but should not be read against published tables collected under the masked or NDC protocols.

We render every held-out view and recompute PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS with the same evaluator used for our rows.
