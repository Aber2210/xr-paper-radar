3.1 Gaussian Mapping Backbone
We adopt SplaTAM  [ 11 ] as the reconstruction backbone with ActiveGS-style
active planning infrastructure  [ 10 ] .
Habitat  [ 25 ] provides synchronized RGB-D frames with ground-truth camera
poses.
In both Replica and Matterport3D experiments, these poses are used as oracle SLAM input
(Sec.  4.1 ).
The scene is represented by isotropic 3D Gaussians optimized through differentiable rendering
with RGB and depth L 1 L_{1} losses over silhouette pixels  [ 11 , 10 ] .
Following ActiveGS  [ 10 ] , we maintain a voxel-based exploration map for collision-free candidate
pose sampling and execute selected views with an RRT-based local planner  [ 16 ] .

Datasets.
We evaluate in Habitat  [ 25 ] on two photorealistic indoor benchmarks.
All methods in our comparisons receive the same ground-truth Habitat camera poses; none is
evaluated with estimated poses or SLAM localization noise.
Replica   [ 27 ] : eight scenes ( office0 – office4 ,
room0 – room2 ).
Replica follows the standard ActiveGS  [ 10 ] /ActiveSGM  [ 4 ]
benchmark and therefore enables direct comparison with previous active mapping work.
Following ActiveGS  [ 10 ] /ActiveSGM  [ 4 ] , each Replica
exploration is limited to 2000 steps.
RGB-D is captured at 680 × 1200 680\times 1200 .
Novel-view semantic evaluation uses held-out camera views that are not used for mapping.
Matterport3D (MP3D)   [ 2 ] : five scenes from the benchmark
used by NARUTO  [ 6 ] , ActiveGAMER  [ 3 ] , and
ActiveSGM  [ 4 ] . The scenes are GdvgFV5R1Z5 ,
gZ6f7yhEvPG , HxpKQynjfin , pLe4wQe7qrG , and
YmJkqBEsHnH .
We use the same Habitat rendering settings as on Replica.
Novel-view semantic evaluation likewise uses held-out camera views that are not used for mapping.
We follow the MP3D protocol of ActiveSGM  [ 4 ] ,
ActiveGAMER  [ 3 ] , and NARUTO  [ 6 ] using the same five
benchmark scenes and a 5000-step exploration budget.
No established passive semantic baseline exists for this protocol; therefore, passive
comparisons are reported only on Replica.
Baselines.
Passive (Replica only, semantic evaluation): fixed NVS trajectory, SAM–CLIP
embedding extraction, and offline language-field training.
Geometry-based : geometry-only ActiveGS  [ 10 ] planner with the same
SAM–CLIP and language-field pipeline.
Geometry-based + CorrCLIP : the same geometric planner with CorrCLIP applied to
accepted keyframe masks and embeddings; candidate views are still ranked only by geometry.
ActiveSGM traj. + LangSplatV2 : we replay ActiveSGM’s recorded pose sequence and
keyframe schedule on each scene, replacing OneFormer with our SAM–CLIP extraction and
LangSplatV2 language-field training.
This comparison keeps the open-vocabulary perception stack fixed, but does not isolate
trajectory quality completely because the replay contains 868 steps versus 956 for AOV-GS.
ActiveSGM   [ 4 ] : per-scene OneFormer fine-tuning on closed
Replica and Matterport3D label sets, run in our Habitat setup on both datasets; shown for
context only, not as an open-vocabulary baseline.
AOV-GS (Ours) : our semantic stage-0 planner without CorrCLIP.
AOV-GS + CorrCLIP (Ours) : the same semantic planner with CorrCLIP applied both to
candidate masks during re-ranking and to accepted keyframe masks used for the embedding bank.
Metrics.
Primary: open-vocabulary language-field mIoU  [ 5 ] on text-query
pairs over held-out novel views.
