3.1 Problem Formulation and State Representation
Our goal is to recover the metric 3D motion of both hands and the camera in a common world frame.
We take the camera coordinate system of the first frame as the world frame, so that no external
localization, gravity estimate, or scene template is required at inference time.

4.4 Ablation Study on HOT3D and ARCTIC demonstrate improved world-space accuracy and temporal consistency over state-of-the-art methods. DewHand reduces rigid-aligned world-space error relative to StableHand from 32.9 32.9 to 28.4 28.4  mm on HOT3D and from 76.2 76.2 to 36.2 36.2  mm on ARCTIC. It also recovers camera scale within 10 % 10\% on two thirds of HOT3D clips, versus about a quarter for SLAM. Moreover, in zero-shot evaluations on HoloAssist, H2O, and TACO, captured with different devices, DewHand achieves the lowest rigid-aligned world-space error (WA-MPJPE) on each dataset.
