From a single image, SceneRig recovers objects, supporting surfaces, and their relations, and initializes a gravity-aligned metric scene through physical settling. Geometry, material, and lighting agents construct the surrounding scene. Finally, a pose-refinement agent inspects paired reference/render crops and invokes geometry- and physics-grounded tools to correct object placement.

3.1 Relational and Metric Initialization
We first recover which entities are present, how they support one another, and where they lie in metric 3D. This relational and geometric evidence guides object reconstruction and physical settling.
Relational parsing.
A VLM proposes movable objects 𝒪 \mathcal{O} , root surfaces ℛ \mathcal{R} , and their support and structural relations, with a distinct description for each instance. MolmoPoint [ molmopoint ] localizes each entity from its description, and its predicted points select instance masks from SAM3 [ sam3 ] candidates for that category. Subsequent checks recover objects omitted from the initial inventory, repair split or merged masks, and revise root surfaces and their relations. For occluded objects, we optionally remove foreground occluders with LanPaint [ zheng2025lanpaint ] and re-segment the target. Entity identities persist throughout reconstruction (Appendix ).
Metric initialization.
The scene graph tells us what is present and how entities are related, but not their metric placement in 3D. We use MoGe-2 [ wang2025moge2 ] to estimate camera intrinsics and a dense metric 3D point map. When metric depth and calibrated intrinsics are available, such as from a robot’s RGB-D camera, they replace these estimates. A plane fitted to the main support defines a gravity-aligned coordinate frame in which the support lies at z = 0 z=0 . 4.1 Experimental Setup
Baselines.
We compare with four baselines: VIGA [ viga2026 ] , REST3D [ ma2026rest3d ] , SceneConductor [ kim2026sceneconductor ] , and SimFoundry [ simfoundry2026 ] . To support object-centric evaluation and simulation, we augment VIGA’s system prompt with a simple object-grouping contract: all parts of each movable object share a single empty Blender parent. We also evaluate SAM3D ∗ , which shares SceneRig ’s segmentation, camera, objects, and scene context but retains SAM3D’s predicted poses without our metric grounding, physical settling, or pose refinement. We use Claude Opus 5 as the default VLM wherever applicable. With GPT-6 Astra, we also compare against Codex, a general-purpose harness that reconstructs the scene directly in Blender without external models.
