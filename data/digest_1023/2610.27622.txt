Which camera. Design.
Eight policies (Table  1 ) span three views: the head camera, a wrist fisheye and a fixed external camera. Teleoperated actions come from the encoders except in arm 6, and glove labels from the head camera unless stated. The teleoperator watched the head-camera preview and all three cameras recorded every episode, so the arms share demonstration trajectories and differ only in observation and data composition. Arms 1–4 test co-training at two teleoperation budgets (172 and 48 episodes); arm 6 tests head-camera labels in place of encoders; arms 5, 7 and 8 test the view: arm 5 is the wrist-camera UMI recipe [ 7 ] with a fisheye added to the glove for this ablation only, arm 7 a fixed camera ( 10   % 10\text{\,}\mathrm{\%} fewer glove frames to body occlusion), and arm 8 head labels on the wrist view, separating the view from the labels.
