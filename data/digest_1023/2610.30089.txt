Common metrics.
Predictions are matched and scored separately at the two proactive levels.
Predictions at both levels are matched to ground truth by Hungarian
matching  ( Kuhn, 1955 ) , which requires that an LLM-judge’s semantic
similarity exceeds a threshold and that the temporal offset falls within the
matching window; among admitted edges the assignment minimizes the cost
α ⁡ ( 1 − sim ) + ( 1 − α ) ​ ( | Δ | / W ) p \alpha(1-\mathrm{sim})+(1-\alpha)\,(|\Delta|/W)^{p}   ( Zhang et al., 2025c ) . Intervention
predictions are first converted into intervals and matched on temporal
overlap. Three metric groups are then shared by the two levels:
(1)  Timing (Start@ τ \tau ): the fraction of matched actions whose
start falls within various τ \tau seconds of the ground-truth moment. We
report Start@0.2 as the main result: the 200 ms tolerance reflects the
residual inter-annotator disagreement on notification timing
(Table  3 ) and is widely used in real-time streaming
models.
(2)  Abs Error ( | Δ s | |\Delta_{s}| , σ \sigma ): the mean absolute start
offset and its standard deviation, in seconds, measuring how far and how
consistently a model deviates from the annotated moment.
(3)  Detection (Prec, Recall, F1): precision over emitted actions,
recall over ground-truth events, and their harmonic mean F1,
penalizing spurious interruptions and missed ones.
Intervention -level specific metrics.
The Intervention-level metrics score the lifecycle itself through two further groups:
(4)  Overlap (mIoU): interval IoU averaged over all
ground-truth opportunities, scoring unmatched opportunities as zero, so a high
score requires starting, maintaining, and stopping the tool lifecycle at the
right boundaries; various thresholds in IoU@ τ \tau are also reported.
(5)  QA (Acc): accuracy of the ProactEgoMem-QA dataset requiring
proactive memory.
Baselines.
The comparison includes proprietary models (Gemini 2.5 Flash ( Comanici et al., 2025 ) and
GPT-5.4-mini ( OpenAI, 2026 ) ), general open-source models (Gemma4-31B ( Gemma Team, 2026 ) ,
Qwen3.8-27B ( Qwen Team, Alibaba Group, 2026c ) , Qwen3.6-27B ( Qwen Team, Alibaba Group, 2026b ) ,
Qwen3.5-9B ( Qwen Team, Alibaba Group, 2026a ) ), video-language models (VideoLLaMA3-7B ( Zhang et al., 2025a ) , LLaVA-OV-1.5-8B ( An et al., 2025 ) ,
MiniCPM-o-4.5 ( OpenBMB Team, 2026 ) ), and streaming models (MMDuet ( Wang et al., 2025c ) , VideoLLM-Online ( Chen et al., 2024a ) , Inkling-Small ( Thinking Machines Lab, 2026 ) , and JoyAI-VL ( Yao et al., 2026 ) ).
