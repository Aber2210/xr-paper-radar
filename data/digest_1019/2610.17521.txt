【实验】
setting.
We consider three scenes on two bimanual platforms. Duo and
Flat are scenes on a dual Franka FR3 setup and differ in their table
and background. The third scene uses a TX-G2 (AgiBot-G2-compatible) platform
and is denoted TX-G2 . Both platforms carry a YUBI gripper with a fisheye
camera on each wrist. The task inventory comprises three tasks: Cup (cup
pick-and-place), Tape (placing tape into a box), and Pen (placing a pen into a
box). The current ranking results use all three tasks; evaluation details
appear in Sec.  IV .
Capture.
Reconstructing the environment for two independently moving fisheye wrist
cameras requires visual coverage beyond the immediate manipulation target.
Narrow-angle capture can leave surrounding regions poorly observed even
though they later appear in policy inputs. We therefore use an Insta360
camera to obtain broad angular coverage with a compact source-frame set,
keeping reconstruction optimization practical. Reconstruction uses only its
left fisheye lens ( Insta360 v6 in the camera metadata).
Videos contain two 3840 × 3840 3840\times 3840 tracks at 50 fps (HEVC). Capture durations
are 213.06 s (Duo), 254.88 s (Flat), and 243.50 s (TX-G2).
We partition each video temporally and select one source frame per interval
that maximizes the minimum left/right Laplacian variance, without an explicit
manual blur-rejection threshold. The same source timestamps also support a
controlled comparison between the full fisheye-derived projections and a
central pinhole view (Sec.  V-C ).
Reconstruction.
Using OpenCV fisheye calibration, each source frame is reprojected into nine
2048 × 2048 2048\times 2048 pinhole views with 90 ∘ × 90 ∘ 90^{\circ}\times 90^{\circ} FOV and focal
length 1024 px: one forward view and eight views tilted 55 ∘ 55^{\circ} from
forward, spaced at 45 ∘ 45^{\circ} azimuth intervals.
We estimate poses with COLMAP  Schönberger and Frahm [2016] ; Schönberger et al. [2016] through pycolmap 4.1.1, generate a dense seed
point cloud with OpenMVS Cernea [2020] , and train Gaussian
Wrapping  Gomez et al. [2026] at full resolution for 30,000–60,000 iterations using an NVIDIA H200 GPU.
The initial dense seed is capped at 40 million points with seed 0.
Exposure compensation, normal fields, fast multiview regularization, and
masked rendering loss are enabled.
The single-lens reconstruction has arbitrary scale. A stored transformation
registers it to MuJoCo world coordinates. All embodiments
are manually aligned to robot visual meshes, and the tabletop collision
surface is modeled as the plane z = 0 z=0 .
