3.1 Correspondence-Guided Geometric Optimization
Sparse panoramic inputs provide limited overlap and often contain large
textureless regions, where conventional feature matching yields too few
reliable constraints for 3DGS optimization. We therefore leverage a frozen
feed-forward large reconstruction model (LRM) as an offline correspondence
teacher. As shown in Appendix Tab.  7 , conventional feature-based matchers such as i3dgs  ( Meuleman et al., 2026 ) and COLMAP  ( Pan et al., 2024 ) are insensitive to the number of input views because they operate pairwise, but produce too few reliable correspondences on sparsely textured indoor surfaces. LRMs provide substantially denser matches, although the number of forward–backward-consistent correspondences decreases when additional context views are jointly processed. We therefore select nearby view pairs with sufficient co-visibility and process each pair independently with DA3  ( Lin et al., 2025 ) , yielding a dense set of reliable correspondence anchors for subsequent 3DGS optimization.
