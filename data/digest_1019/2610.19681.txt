Datasets. Following prior works  [ 5 , 3 , 16 , 7 , 10 ] , we evaluate the proposed method on three widely used benchmarks:
( i ) DTU [ 75 ] : The DTU multi-view dataset contains 15 real-world scenes, each captured from 49 or 64 viewpoints with high-resolution images. Each scene contains objects with diverse materials and complex geometries, including both reflectance and non-reflectance surfaces. DTU provides ground-truth 3D models and camera trajectories, making it a standard benchmark for quantitative evaluation of point cloud reconstruction.( ii ) Shiny Blender [ 65 ] : The Shiny Blender dataset is a synthetic benchmark consisting of 6 reflective objects rendered under controlled settings. We use it to provide additional quantitative evaluation on reflective surfaces.
( iii ) Ref-NeRF [ 65 ] : The Ref-NeRF dataset is designed to evaluate reconstruction quality for reflective objects in uncontrolled real-world scenarios. It includes three challenging scenes, i.e., “sedan”, “garden spheres”, and “toycar”, each featuring multi-view images of highly reflective surfaces. Ref-NeRF specifically tests an algorithm’s robustness to specular reflections.
