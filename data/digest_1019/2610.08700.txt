reVISit-XR makes a WebXR stimulus embeddable, trackable, and replayable within a reVISit study.
(1) Researchers build or adapt a WebXR stimulus in the reVISit-XR Stimulus Build Package ,
(2) embed it in a reVISit study through the Study Integration Package (or a study forked from \href https://github.com/revisit-studies/study/the reVISit GitHub repository ↗ \nearrow [ 8 , 6 ] ), then collect participant responses and semantic provenance during headset interaction,
(3) and later inspect the session through semantic state rehydration. We introduced reVISit-XR, an extension of reVISit that makes WebXR stimuli embeddable, trackable, and replayable as first-class study components.
By combining stimulus building and integration, semantic provenance capture, and replay through state rehydration, it makes XR studies easier to deploy, inspect, and reproduce. We see reVISit-XR as one part of a broader effort toward open research infrastructure.
