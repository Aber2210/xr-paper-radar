5.3 Ablation Study We organize evaluation around annotation quality, preliminary training
utility, and key design choices. setup.
Our purpose-built Evaluation Subset covers whole-body motion, hand
articulation, and regional contact. Evaluation addresses three aspects:
kinematic accuracy , through end-point and full-body point errors;
temporal quality , through tracking continuity and trajectory smoothness;
and contact accuracy , through regional IoU and F1. References are
modality-specific, with full-body errors measured against tracker-fitted labels.
The remaining Natural Recordings lack matched accuracy references and are assessed for sensor agreement, temporal quality, and geometric consistency.
Subsets, metrics, and full-body references are detailed in Secs.   F.1 , F.4 and  F.2 .
5.1 Annotation Reliability
Whole-body reconstruction.
Tab.   2 compares three baseline settings: public pretrained
checkpoints with calibrated sensor-to-anchor conversion, direct use of raw
tracker poses, and fine-tuning with five tracker anchors. EgoExplorer outperforms
all three settings across every reported metric, achieving 18.0 mm body-point
error and 20.3 mm at non-tracked points. Its lowest errors extend beyond the
observed anchors to other body points, including elbows and knees, indicating
better recovery of full-body articulation.
