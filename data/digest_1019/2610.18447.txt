8.1.1 EWMBench Evaluation
We first evaluate the quality of the generated world itself. EWMBench  [ 12 ]
is a standard benchmark for embodied world models. It scores a generated video
along eight dimensions covering visual scene consistency, end-effector motion
correctness, and semantic alignment with the instruction, and reports their sum
as Overall. Its standard evaluation set contains 21 episodes drawn from 7
manipulation tasks. For every episode, the model produces three candidate videos
conditioned on the ground-truth first frame and the official instruction, the best
candidate is selected by Hausdorff distance, and each dimension is normalized to [ 0 , 1 ] [0,1] and averaged over all valid trials, 63 videos in total. Generation uses
48 frames at a resolution of 480 × 640 480\times 640 with 50 denoising steps and a
classifier-free guidance scale of 5.0. Since the benchmark computes its metrics
at 30 FPS, generated results are upsampled from 5 FPS to 30 FPS so that the
comparison follows the protocol on which the benchmark is defined.

Thinker-WM: A Unified World Model with Shared Multimodal Backbone for Embodied Intelligence
\productname Thinker-WM and Future Work
This work presented Thinker-WM , a unified multimodal world model that jointly models RGB, depth, and robot action within a shared intermediate representation. Modality-specific encoders and decoders preserve the distinct structure of each modality, while a shared Unified Dynamics Transformer (UDT) models their joint evolution with a single set of weights. This design supports multiple world-action functions under one parameterization, including joint video-action generation, world modeling, policy inference, action-conditioned generation, video-to-action generation, and inverse dynamics. Trained on approximately 20K hours of egocentric video, 10K hours of robot data, and 1K hours of simulation data, about 4K hours with aligned depth, Thinker-WM uses a staged curriculum with replay to stabilize shared representations and specialize to the deployed robot.
