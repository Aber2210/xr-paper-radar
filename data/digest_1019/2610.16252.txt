Pose-Driven 3DGS Realistic Rendering Given the initial poses of a human and an object, the object geometry, and a
textual instruction, our goal is to generate a complete 3D human–object
interaction (HOI) sequence, including whole-body human motion, articulated
finger motion, and object motion over time. As shown in
Figure  2 , MFlowHOI consists of three main modules. In
HOI Latent Representation, we design a contact-aware multipart
RVQ-VAE to encode multipart human motion, object motion, and contact information into a temporal multipart-based HOI latent representation. In Conditional MeanFlow, we employ an ST-DiT with spatial–temporal and cross-part attention to generate the HOI latent sequence in a single transport evaluation, conditioned on the textual instruction, initial poses, and object geometry. In Realistic Rendering, we develop a pose-driven 3D Gaussian Splatting framework to produce high-fidelity renderings of the generated 3D HOI sequences.

In this paper, we propose MFlowHOI, a one-step framework for contact-aware
whole-body HOI generation. Its Contact-Aware Multipart RVQ-VAE decomposes an
interaction into functional body parts, object motion, contact probabilities,
and object-local contact positions. Stream-specific temporal encoders capture
fine-grained dynamics, while a shared decoder preserves global coordination,
forming a structured time–part latent space.
In this latent space, a conditional Spatio-Temporal Diffusion Transformer
(ST-DiT) learns the MeanFlow velocity field. It models temporal dependencies
within each stream and cross-part interactions among the body, hands, object,
and contacts. A single evaluation generates a complete interaction latent,
which is decoded into whole-body and finger motion, object trajectories, and
contact cues. The resulting contact correspondences also enable geometric
refinement and interaction-preserving transfer to photorealistic 3D Gaussian
avatars with different body proportions. on GRAB demonstrate improved distributional quality and
text–motion alignment with substantially faster inference than existing HOI
generators. Ablations confirm the importance of the multipart latent space and its temporal and cross-part modeling.
The main contribution of our method can be summarized as follows.
1) We present a new one-step framework that jointly generates coordinated whole-body and finger motion, object trajectories, and time-varying contact cues with a single network.
2) To enable stable conditional MeanFlow generation, we design a temporal multipart–based HOI latent representation to model temporal dependencies between different body parts and objects, and introduce a spatio-temporal diffusion transformer to balance heterogeneous HOI components.
3) We predict contact correspondences between object and body parts that support geometric refinement and interaction-preserving transfer to photorealistic 3D Gaussian avatars with different body proportions. Evaluations

We presented MFlowHOI, a one-step framework for whole-body HOI generation. Its contact-aware multipart codec balances body, hand, object, and contact signals while preserving global coordination. Operating on the resulting time–part latent grid, ST-DiT generates complete interactions with a single MeanFlow evaluation. Experiments demonstrate improved distributional quality and text alignment compared with other generators, at substantially lower latency. Predicted object-local contacts further facilitate efficient geometric correction and avatar deployment. Although promising results were achieved, our evaluation is limited to interactions with a single rigid object. Extending the method to articulated/deformable objects remains future work.
