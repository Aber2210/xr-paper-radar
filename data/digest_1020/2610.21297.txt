Training Datasets.
For training, we use Holo360D ( Ou et al., 2026 ) , a large-scale real-world panoramic 3D dataset comprising 75 indoor and outdoor scenes. It provides panoramic RGB images with corresponding depth maps, each captured at a resolution of 2880 × 1440. We train our model using 23,000 panoramic frames.   Datasets and Evaluation Metrics.
For evaluation, following prior work ( Lin et al., 2025 ) , we test our model on three 360 ∘ depth datasets: Matterport3D ( Chang et al., 2017 ) (indoor) and Stanford2D3D ( Armeni et al., 2017 ) (indoor), and Deep360 ( Li et al., 2022a ) (outdoor).
To comprehensively assess model performance, we evaluate both point cloud using 3D metrics ( Jung et al., 2025 ) such as Chamfer Distance, F-Score, and Intersection over Union (IoU), and depth using standard 2D metrics including Absolute Relative Error (AbsRel), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and the accuracy under a threshold δ i \delta_{i} with i i =1.25. .
As reported in Tab.  1 , GeoPano establishes a new state-of-the-art, demonstrating superior performance across all 3D and 2D metrics. Additionally, as illustrated in Fig.  5 and Fig.  6 , our approach significantly mitigates spherical distortions and preserves geometric consistency, yielding high-fidelity 3D structures across diverse indoor and outdoor environments.
