Main results.
Table  3 shows that VISTA improves the overall score of every backbone. The largest gains are for InternVL3-2B (2.75 to 3.54, +28.7% ) and Qwen3-VL-4B (3.22 to 3.95, +22.7% ), followed by Qwen3-VL-8B ( +18.7% ) and InternVL3-8B ( +17.7% ); for these four models, all rubric components and all three response forms improve. With VISTA, Qwen3-VL-4B obtains the highest score in our evaluation, above both the best zero-shot open-source model, InternVL3-14B (3.33), and the best proprietary model (3.75) in Table  2 . Guidance improves the most, from 1.96 to 3.28 for InternVL3-2B and from 2.65 to 3.69 for Qwen3-VL-4B. In InternVL3 and Qwen3-VL, the combined adaptation and sensor-fusion procedure produces larger score gains for the smaller of the two evaluated backbones, and after adaptation Qwen3-VL-4B matches Qwen3-VL-8B (3.95 vs. 3.93).
Task-level trade-offs.
Gemma 3-12B shows a smaller gain, from 3.07 to 3.24 (+5.5%). Its guidance score improves from 2.48 to 3.32 and accessible communication from 0.36 to 0.43, whereas scene description and question answering drop from 3.41 to 3.00 and from 3.76 to 3.30. The adapted Gemma 3 model therefore shifts toward guidance at the cost of descriptive and question-answering quality, unlike the InternVL3 and Qwen3-VL models, which improve on all three response forms. 5.3 Multimodal Enhancement
Main results.
Task-level trade-offs. Setting
VISTA evaluates three response forms: scene description, visual question answering, and goal-conditioned guidance. We test InternVL3  ( Zhu et al., 2025 ) , Qwen3-VL  ( Bai et al., 2025 ) , Gemma 3  ( Gemma Team et al., 2025 ) , and VideoLLaMA3  ( Zhang et al., 2025a ) across model scales, together with Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite  ( Google, 2026 ) and GPT-Realtime Mini  ( OpenAI, 2025 ) as proprietary zero-shot baselines. All experiments share the VISTA test split, task prompts, eight uniformly sampled frames, and a maximum output length of 4,096 tokens. As an external reference, we evaluate ten of these models (eight open-source and both proprietary) on EgoBlind (setup in Appendix  B , results in Appendix  C.2 ).
The evaluation protocol is shared by the RGB zero-shot baselines and the multimodal model experiments. GPT-6 Luna  ( OpenAI, 2026 ) scores each prediction against the input frames and reference annotations on evidence grounding (0–1.75), answer correctness (0–1.75), task completion (0–1), and accessible communication (0–0.5), yielding a total from 0 to 5. Predictions containing potentially harmful instructions are flagged, with the final score capped at 1.95. The multimodal experiments change only the available sensor inputs or adaptation configuration; prompts, test data, judge, and rubric remain fixed. Appendix  B gives the judge prompt, inference settings, and reproducibility details.
