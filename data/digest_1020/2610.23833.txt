4.2.1 Preparation 4.1. Participants
4.1.1. Recruitment Process
This study recruited participants through online social media. The
research team posted recruitment notices in three autistic adult related
social media communities and under relevant hashtags. Inclusion
criteria were as follows. Participants had to be at least 18 years
old. They needed to hold an autism diagnosis certificate issued by a
formal medical or psychological assessment institution. They had to
be able to complete informed consent independently. They could not
have uncorrected severe visual or auditory impairment, since the
prototype experience involved visual imagery and spatial audio
presentation. They could not have a history of photosensitive
epilepsy or other contraindications for XR device use. Exclusion
criteria included the following. Participants with a clear prior
history of adverse reactions to head mounted displays, vibration
patches, or temperature patches were excluded. Those currently in an
acute mental health crisis were excluded. Those unable to understand
experimental instructions or independently express their sensory
experience were also excluded.
4.1.2. Recruitment Results
The recruitment received a total of 25 sign up submissions. After
initial screening, which included verification of diagnosis
certificates, confirmation of age range, and checking for device use
contraindications, 20 participants ultimately met all inclusion
criteria and completed informed consent, entering the formal
experimental process. Participant ages ranged from 19 to 55 years,
with 10 males and 10 females, an even distribution. Ten participants
held a bachelor’s degree, six held a high school education, three
held a middle school education, and one held only an elementary
school education, covering a fairly wide range from elementary
school to bachelor’s degree. The sample showed a degree of
heterogeneity in age, gender, and education level. Detailed
information is shown in Table  2 .

4.2. Intervention Procedure
This study completed all data collection through a single laboratory
visit, with each participant’s total participation time controlled
to approximately 90 to 110 minutes, in order to avoid sensory
fatigue or attention decline in autistic participants during long
tasks. The overall procedure was divided into four stages. (1) Pre
test preparation and adaptation. (2) Sequential experience of the
five interaction conditions with immediate measurement. (3) A
retrospective interview after all conditions were completed. (4) , and that the purpose was simply to understand the
