V Experiments
Extensive experiments are conducted to demonstrate that: (1) the omnidirectional FoV and the proposed panoramic feature extraction provide stronger feature constraints and improve performance; (2) the proposed panoramic loop closure corrects
accumulated drift and improves global consistency; and (3) the evaluation of our panoramic VI-SLAM on an embedded platform demonstrates its feasibility for onboard UAV deployment.
