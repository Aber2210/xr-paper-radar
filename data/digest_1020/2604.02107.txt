We evaluate all methods on the same server equipped with an Intel Xeon Gold 6342 CPU and an NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU with 24GB VRAM to ensure a consistent evaluation. Experiments are conducted on EuRoC MAV  [ 38 ] and TUM RGB-D for indoor scenes, and on KITTI  [ 39 ] for large-scale outdoor scenarios. We report ATE on EuRoC MAV and TUM RGB-D, and the relative pose error metrics on KITTI. For monocular methods, all trajectory-based metrics are computed after 7-DoF Sim(3) alignment with the ground-truth trajectory. All results are reported as mean ± \pm standard deviation over five runs, where “ < 0.01 <0.01 ” denotes standard deviation below the displayed precision in the table. For methods that provide official loop-closure options, we use their official source-code interfaces to enable or disable these modules. In the tables, “w/o LC” denotes odometry-only results, while “w/ LC” denotes results with loop closure when available. We also include a method, VO+DA3 , in Tabs.  I – III to evaluate a simpler design that directly combines the classical VO pose with Depth-Anything 3.
