3.3 Segment-Level MOPD Figure 2: Overview of EgoP 2 C.
The framework integrates planning, perception, and composition into a single VLM.
(a) Planner (PC) and Perception (V) teachers share a frozen backbone and use separate LoRA adapters trained with SFT followed by GRPO.
(b) Teacher-generated sequences initialize corresponding MoE experts through separate role training followed by joint SFT.
z t z_{t} denotes the t t -th token.
(c) Segment-level MOPD trains the student on its own interactions, with PC supervising subquestions and composition and V supervising observations.
(d) At inference, the Planner expert either answers directly using the General expert ( DIRECT ) or iteratively requests subquestion answers from Perception before composing the final answer ( PLAN ).
Given an input x = ( v , q ) x{=}(v,q) , which consists of an egocentric video v v and a spatial question q q , the task is to predict the corresponding answer a a .
Under our protocol, the question is answered either directly or by following a plan-perceive-compose process.
The Planner first selects a mode
b ∈ { DIRECT , PLAN } b\in\{\texttt{DIRECT},\texttt{PLAN}\} .
With DIRECT , the model answers the original question without requesting observations.
With PLAN , the Planner requests spatial observations over one or more rounds before composing the answer.
At round j j , it generates a request u j u_{j} containing one or more self-contained spatial subquestions.
Perception answers each from the video, producing a list of textual observations o j o_{j} .
The Planner uses the accumulated requests and observations to either request further subquestions or compose the final answer.
Perception receives only the video and its current subquestion.
Let J J denote the number of planning rounds and c c the final composition, including the answer a a .
The output is y = a y{=}a for DIRECT and y = ( u 1 , o 1 , … , u J , o J , c ) y{=}(u_{1},o_{1},\ldots,u_{J},o_{J},c) for PLAN .
Appendix  B.3 provides the full execution details and probabilistic formulation.
Figure  2 (a-c) summarizes three training stages.
We train Planner and Perception teachers in a Dual-LoRA configuration (Section  3.1 ).
These teachers provide supervision for initializing the corresponding roles in a MoE student (Section  3.2 ).
We then apply segment-level MOPD, where each teacher supervises its corresponding segments of student-generated trajectories (Section  3.3 ).
At inference, the student’s Planner expert selects the General expert for direct answering or iteratively asks the Perception expert to answer subquestions before composing the final answer (Figure  2 (d)).
