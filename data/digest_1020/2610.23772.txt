Ethics Statement
VDAs turn a single photograph into a photo-realistic, controllable video of
the depicted person. The same capability enables impersonation and
non-consensual synthetic media. We train and evaluate only on multi-view
datasets whose participants gave informed consent and which their authors
released for research use
(NeRSemble  ( Kirschstein et al., 2023 ) ,
AVA-256  ( Martinez et al., 2024 ) ,
BecomingLit  ( Schmidt et al., 2025 ) ,
Multiface  ( Wuu et al., 2022 ) ) and on the public VFHQ
benchmark  ( Xie et al., 2022 ) .
