3 Preliminaries
We review denoising diffusion probabilistic models for 3D point cloud generation and then introduce the Hilbert curve as the locality-preserving space-filling curve used in our conditioning framework.

Overall, these additional-category experiments support the
effectiveness of HMC conditioning with either a learned
condition prior or a training-set condition bank. The VAE
variant achieves better CD-based 1-NNA on both categories,
while the bank variant achieves higher EMD-based coverage
on both and higher CD-based coverage on Vessel.
