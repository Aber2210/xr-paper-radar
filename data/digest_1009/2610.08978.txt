We process a stream of images sequentially. At step i i , the incoming image 𝐈 i ∈ ℝ H × W × 3 \mathbf{I}_{i}\in\mathbb{R}^{H\times W\times 3} is used to update the latent scene state and produce the current accumulated Gaussian reconstruction.
As shown in Fig. 2 , we first encode 𝐈 i \mathbf{I}_{i} into frame tokens 𝐟 i \mathbf{f}_{i} 1 1

Such frame tokens are the representations implicitly encoded in their own frame’s camera coordinate system. . Secondly, we feed the existing latent spatial tokens 𝐒 i − 1 \mathbf{S}_{i-1} from the last step and the newly encoded 𝐟 i \mathbf{f}_{i} into the proposed Stream-Spatial (SS) Transformer. In the SS-Transformer, 𝐒 i − 1 \mathbf{S}_{i-1} is adapted to the new frames and viewpoints; meanwhile, 𝐟 i \mathbf{f}_{i} is implicitly aligned to the latent spatial space.
We then apply the admission module to the updated 𝐟 i ′ \mathbf{f}^{\prime}_{i} and 𝐒 i − 1 ′ \mathbf{S}^{\prime}_{i-1} , and append the selected frame tokens to form 𝐒 i ∈ ℝ ( M + δ ) × D \mathbf{S}_{i}\in\mathbb{R}^{(M+\delta)\times D} ,
where δ \delta is the number of admitted frame tokens. We then provide 𝐒 i \mathbf{S}_{i} and token sets from two other layers of the SS-Transformer as inputs to the Hierarchical Decoder and predict Gaussian candidates with the Gaussian Head. Each token corresponds to h h Gaussians. Finally, 𝐒 i \mathbf{S}_{i} is carried over to the next step.

4.1 Ablation Study We train S2Tok solely on DL3DV  ( Ling et al., 2024 ) and RealEstate10K  ( Zhou et al., 2018 ) . More training, testing, and implementation details can be found in Sec.  D in the Appendix.
For evaluations, we show extensive quantitative and qualitative comparisons between S2Tok and other baselines.
We get the rendering performance of pointmap prediction methods using point-splat rendering  ( Zwicker et al., 2001 ; Pfister et al., 2000 ) .
Specifically, we hold out one target view between consecutive context
views and evaluate reconstruction quality in two settings:
1) Final : Render all target views using the final
reconstruction after processing the whole context sequence.
2) Intermediate : After each step, render the
corresponding target view using the current reconstruction,
based only on the context views observed so far.
Streaming methods update the scene incrementally, whereas non-streaming step. StreamSplat is evaluated only under Intermediate , as its
time-conditioned reconstruction does not produce a globally
camera-aligned final scene.
