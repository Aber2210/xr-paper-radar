We provide detailed definitions of the image reconstruction and geometric
metrics used in our experiments as follows.
Image Reconstruction Quality.
Following ReconDrive  Yu et al. [2026a] , we report PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS
for both visual scene reconstruction and novel-view synthesis. Let I ^ \hat{I}
and I I denote the rendered and reference images, respectively, and let M M
