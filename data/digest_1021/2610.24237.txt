3.5 Ratings and TMT-VR performance 3.1 Sample characteristics
Table 1 presents the sample characteristics overall and by age group, with the age-group comparisons. The age groups did not differ significantly in sex composition, education or motion sickness susceptibility. Younger participants reported significantly more gaming and technology experience (both Holm p < .001; Table 1). Gaming scores were positively skewed in the older group, with 52 participants (57.1%) across the sample obtaining the minimum score. Reported VR use was minimal in both groups: 89 of the 91 participants gave the lowest frequency rating for VR, and 41 of the 44 older participants reported no VR ability. Motion sickness susceptibility, which entered the analyses as a predictor, ranged from 0 to 29.5 on the 0–54 scale and had similar means in the two groups (Table 1).
