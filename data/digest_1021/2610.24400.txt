5.1 Methodology
Dataset.
Images.cv (26k images, 3 × 256 × 256 3{\times}256{\times}256 , two
categories) is our primary benchmark with a
70/15/15% train/val/test split.
CelebA-HQ 256   Karras et al. (2017) ; Liu et al. (2015)
(28k faces) provides a zero-shot cross-dataset generalization test; models are evaluated on it without any fine-tuning of the Images.cv-trained weights.
Hyperparameters.
To train our models we use AdamW  Loshchilov and Hutter (2017) with cosine annealing and
warm restarts  Loshchilov and Hutter (2016) ; AMP (automatic mixed precision)  Micikevicius et al. (2017) enabled.
On Images.cv at 256 × 256 256{\times}256 : batch  32 32 , lr  10 − 3 10^{-3} ,
min-lr  10 − 6 10^{-6} , weight decay  10 − 4 10^{-4} , warmup  5 5 epochs,
100 100 total epochs. in Optics 3 , pp. 100072 .
