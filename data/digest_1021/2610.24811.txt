. The offset γ shift \gamma_{\mathrm{shift}} that sets the initialization of the illumination sigmoid is hand-picked per environment, and its sensitivity remains unexplored. A learned or data-driven initialization would remove this manual choice. While the factorized albedo and the learned illumination field are theoretically separable, practical relighting under arbitrary (non-capture) illumination depends on the completeness of the irradiance sampling during capture.
Future work. We will explore environment map extraction from the learned SH coefficients and evaluate it under synthetic and real relighting conditions. Also, our current formulation assumes Lambertian (diffuse) reflectance. Highly reflective surfaces would require explicit specular modeling, e.g. view-dependent specular terms observed in endoscopy.
