【实验】
 We evaluate camera, depth, hand, and object reconstruction on HOT3D,
EgoX-Bench, H2O, and HOI4D using identical 150-observation clips
across methods. Evaluation sequences are excluded from EgoX training.
EgoX uses RGB and first-frame object masks; object baselines may
additionally require estimated depth, intrinsics, per-frame masks,
or metric CAD assets. World-space hand metrics use estimated cameras;
object poses are evaluated in camera coordinates. No method receives
GT camera poses or scale in the main comparisons.
Appendix  C details the evaluation protocols. on EgoX-Bench and public benchmarks demonstrate
strong performance in hand and object reconstruction
and depth estimation.
Our current formulation assumes rigid objects and does not
predict their meshes.
Future work will explore object mesh prediction and
multiview inputs.

F Training and Compute
Training supervision. We present EgoXperience, a unified approach to learning
world-grounded egocentric representations through task-driven
capture, geometrically aligned annotation, and joint reconstruction.
EgoX-Engine provides aligned multimodal annotations for
the 50-hour EgoX-Data-6M, from which we curate the held-out
one-hour EgoX-Bench.
Trained on these annotations and harmonized public datasets,
EgoX jointly reconstructs cameras, dense depth, hands,
and rigid-object 6-DoF poses in a common metric world frame.
