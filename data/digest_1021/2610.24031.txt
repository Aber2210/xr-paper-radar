3.1. Overview
We propose SynerGest , a high-fidelity co-speech gesture generator built on Contrastive Flow Matching (CFM), designed to model the interaction within a dyadic conversation. The generator synthesizes motion 𝐱 ∈ ℝ T × D \mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times D} for the target avatar (acting as the listener in our joint-modeling scenario), where T T denotes the sequence length and D D represents the modality-specific spatial dimension. We first extract raw audio waveforms from both the target listener ( 𝐚 l ​ s ​ n \mathbf{a}_{lsn} ) and the conversational partner ( 𝐚 s ​ p ​ k \mathbf{a}_{spk} ). These are denoted as 𝐀 = ( 𝐚 l ​ s ​ n , 𝐚 s ​ p ​ k ) \mathbf{A}=(\mathbf{a}_{lsn},\mathbf{a}_{spk}) and jointly condition generation. A shared, pretrained wav2vec 2.0 backbone combined with mel-spectrogram features processes the two channels independently. To accommodate distinct kinetic properties, the flow estimator separates body and facial-expression pathways (“PoseOnly” and “FaceOnly” in Fig.  2 ). Rather than using a stochastic diffusion chain, we learn a step-size-conditioned velocity field v θ ​ ( 𝐱 t , t , d , 𝐜 ) v_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t,d,\mathbf{c}) , where t t is the absolute flow time and d d is the requested integration step size. The field transports samples from a Gaussian prior p 0 ​ ( 𝐱 ) = 𝒩 ​ ( 0 , I ) p_{0}(\mathbf{x})=\mathcal{N}(0,I) to the empirical distribution p 1 ​ ( 𝐱 ) p_{1}(\mathbf{x}) . For fixed initial noise and audio, integrating from t = 0 t=0 to 1 1 gives a deterministic ODE trajectory; drawing a fresh initial sample yields a different plausible gesture for the same audio.
3.2. Conditional Feature Generation and Fusion
As illustrated in our overall architecture, the conditional pipeline follows a structured progression from raw unimodal extraction to multimodal interactive alignment. First, the raw audio waveforms of the target listener ( 𝐚 l ​ s ​ n \mathbf{a}_{lsn} ) and the speaker ( 𝐚 s ​ p ​ k \mathbf{a}_{spk} ) are independently routed through the frozen AudioRhythm Encoder . These embeddings are concatenated to jointly encode the dyadic conversational context, forming the base acoustic feature 𝐅 a \mathbf{F}_{a} . Crucially, to accommodate their distinct kinetic properties, our framework adopts a decoupled extraction strategy for body pose and facial expressions. 4.4 Ablation Studies 4.1. Experimental Settings
Datasets. We evaluate the proposed SynerGest framework on two extensive dyadic interaction datasets: the Audio2Photoreal dataset  ( Ng and others, 2024 ) and Seamless Interaction  ( Agrawal and others, 2025 ) . Audio2Photoreal offers high-fidelity, studio-captured dyadic conversations paired with photorealistic Codec Avatar assets. The acoustic input comprises 48 kHz dual-channel audio, explicitly capturing the independent waveforms of both the speaker and the listener. Additionally, we utilize the Naturalistic subset of the Seamless Interaction dataset, which encompasses unscripted, spontaneous recordings. For Seamless, the release provides a single-channel track for each participant. Using the interaction-partner mapping, we pair the target and partner tracks, resample them to 48 kHz, align them to their common duration, and stack them as a dual-channel input. Metrics. We adopt four metrics to comprehensively evaluate our framework: Fréchet Gesture Distance (FGD ↓ \downarrow ) for kinematic realism, Beat Consistency (BC ↑ \uparrow ) for audio-motion synchronization, Diversity (Div ↑ \uparrow ) for motion variance, and Inference Efficiency ( 𝒯 inf \mathcal{T}_{\text{inf}} ↓ \downarrow ), defined as the average wall-clock inference time per sample.
Baselines. To rigorously demonstrate the superiority of our framework, we compare SynerGest against four recent state-of-the-art co-speech gesture generation methods. Specifically, we evaluate against three advanced monadic frameworks—EMAGE  ( Liu and others, 2024 ) , GestureLSM  ( Liu et al., 2025b ) , and TalkSHOW  ( Yi and others, 2023 ) —alongside one dedicated dyadic baseline, the Audio2Photoreal (A2P) model  ( Ng and others, 2024 ) . To ensure a fair comparison, all monadic baselines are adapted to our dyadic evaluation protocol under identical data processing conditions.
