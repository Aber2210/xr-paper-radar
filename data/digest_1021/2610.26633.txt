IV-A RGB-D datasets
The proposed system was tested on sequences from two complementary datasets, to evaluate its robustness under different types of dynamics:
•
Bonn RGB-D Dynamic Dataset  [ 20 ] contains highly dynamic scenes with moving people and manipulated objects. In particular the nonobstructing_box sequences show a person moving a little box around, and the obstructing_box sequences show two people moving a wide box that can fully cover the camera view;
•
Panoptic Dataset  [ 11 ] , containing quadruped robots and people.
These two datasets include objects with different appearance, size and motion characteristics. Moreover, quadruped robots do not belong to any COCO classes used to train the panoptic network, and thus appear as unknown moving objects, allowing us to assess the class-agnostic capabilities of our system.
