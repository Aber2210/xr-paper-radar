G.2 Baseline Configurations
We use HandNeRF++ as a radiance-field-based hand
reconstruction baseline, GaussianHand as a generalizable
hand-avatar baseline, and EVA as an optimization-based
monocular Gaussian-avatar baseline. GaussianHand performs one-shot fitting for
100 optimization steps on each target sequence. For EVA, we replace its body
template with MANO to adapt it to hand reconstruction. We otherwise retain the
official configurations and implementations of all baselines.
The baselines use hand poses and projected MANO silhouettes obtained from
WiLoR during per-sequence fitting. For Hand-4DGS, WiLoR poses are used as
pseudo-labels during offline training but are not provided as network inputs
during inference. The projected WiLoR MANO silhouette is used only for alpha
supervision during test-time optimization. For all methods, we use SAM3 masks
to identify visible hand regions and handle hand–object occlusions during
image supervision.
