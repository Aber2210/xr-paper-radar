Prototype Implementation
We implemented NavSight on iOS because iOS devices are most accessible and most widely used by BLV people ( Griffin-Shirley et al., 2017 ) .
We used Unity 2022.3.20f1 for video capture, interface, and augmentation rendering, and Apple’s Core ML ( Apple Inc., 2025 ) for on-device object inference. From the video stream, NavSight captures the video frame, detects objects with our fine-tuned model, and renders the selected augmentations in Unity.
NavSight will be open-sourced upon publication.
